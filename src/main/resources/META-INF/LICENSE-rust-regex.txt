The rust-regex-* test fixtures include patterns and texts from the test suite
of the Rust regex crate (https://github.com/rust-lang/regex), tag 1.13.1, and
results recorded from the crate. RustRegexp follows the pattern syntax and the
Unicode property naming rules of the crate and its regex-syntax 0.8.11 crate,
whose property names come from the crate's table generator, ucd-generate
(https://github.com/BurntSushi/ucd-generate), by the crate's author, and its
ucd-parse and ucd-util crates. All of these are dual-licensed under the MIT
license and the Apache License 2.0. Regulator's search of large literal
alternations ports the automaton of the same author's aho-corasick crate
(https://github.com/BurntSushi/aho-corasick), version 1.1.5, which is
dual-licensed under the Unlicense and the MIT license. Regulator uses them
under the MIT license, reproduced below: the first notice covers the regex
crates and ucd-generate, the second ucd-parse, ucd-util, and aho-corasick. The
third notice covers the AT&T testregex data that the fowler/* fixture records
derive from. The Unicode data license for the property names is in
LICENSE-unicode.txt.
